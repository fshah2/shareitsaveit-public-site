How ShareItSaveIt works

A simple iOS app for people who want to save Instagram posts, TikTok videos, YouTube links, articles, and other web content for later.

ShareItSaveIt is a private link organizer for iPhone. When you find something worth saving, like a TikTok video, an Instagram post, an X thread, a YouTube link, a Reddit post, or a page in Safari, you can use the iPhone Share Sheet to save it in one tap. From there, you can organize your saved links into folders, browse them later without distraction, and keep your bookmarks available offline on your device.


This makes ShareItSaveIt useful for saving recipes, travel ideas, shopping research, creator content, project inspiration, study resources, and anything else you want to revisit. Unlike cloud-heavy bookmarking tools, ShareItSaveIt stays lightweight and private by design.
